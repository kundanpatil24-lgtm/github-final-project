# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment.

## Our Standards
Participants should be respectful, constructive, and professional.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and project-related activities.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior may be addressed by project maintainers.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers will review reports fairly and take appropriate action.
